# August 10 — Animal Kingdom and Hollywood Studios

**Status:** 🟡 Sunlight/mobile prototype

## Daily mission

Experience Animal Kingdom’s signature wildlife, simulator, trails, and live entertainment, then use reserved access for a focused Hollywood Studios afternoon.

!!! info "Day-of controls"
    The floating display control at the lower-right offers **Sunlight**, **Light**, and **Dark** modes. Sunlight mode is the default and is stored in this browser.

!!! warning "Map limitation"
    **Locate** opens a Google Maps search for the attraction. **Navigate** requests walking directions from the phone’s current location. Google may identify the correct general location but not the exact queue entrance or current park routing. Follow park signs and My Disney Experience when they disagree.

## Food plan

### Early breakfast

🥇 Light portable breakfast at Animal Kingdom Lodge or while waiting for the bus.  
🥈 Resort quick service if open early enough.  
🥉 Fruit or protein bar so Early Entry is not sacrificed.

### Morning snack

Choose the nearest practical option after Safari/Gorilla Falls or before Lion King. Do not cross the park for a particular snack.

### Lunch

[Tiffins](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/animal-kingdom/tiffins/) at **1:00 p.m.**

### Hollywood Studios snack

🥇 Choose something near the current Lightning Lane route.  
🥈 Mobile-order during a return-window gap.  
🥉 Defer food until Boma unless needed.

### Dinner

[Boma](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/animal-kingdom-lodge/boma-flavors-of-africa/) at **7:00 p.m.**
