```text

   ▼
Pandora
   │ about 8–12 min to Africa
   ▼
Africa: Safari → Gorilla Falls → Lion King
   │ about 10–15 min to Asia
   ▼
Asia: Everest → optional Maharajah
   │ about 8–12 min to Discovery Island
   ▼
Discovery Island: Zootopia / Tiffins
   │ park-to-park bus
   ▼
Hollywood Studios
```

### Early breakfast

🥇 Light portable breakfast at Animal Kingdom Lodge or while waiting for the bus.  
🥈 Resort quick service if open early enough.

### Hollywood Studios snack

🥇 Choose something near the current Lightning Lane route.  
🥈 Mobile-order during a return-window gap.  
🥉 Defer food until Boma unless needed.

### Dinner

[Boma](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/animal-kingdom-lodge/boma-flavors-of-africa/) at **7:00 p.m.**
