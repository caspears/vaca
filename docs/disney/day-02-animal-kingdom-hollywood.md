# August 10 — Animal Kingdom and Hollywood Studios

## Daily mission

Combine Animal Kingdom’s wildlife, simulator, trails, and live entertainment with a focused Hollywood Studios afternoon.

## Geographic flow

```text
Entrance → Pandora → Africa → Asia → Discovery Island/Tiffins
                                      ↓
                           Bus to Hollywood Studios
```

## Animal Kingdom sequence

1. [Na’vi River Journey](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/attractions/animal-kingdom/navi-river-journey/)
2. Walk Pandora → Africa: allow 8–12 minutes
3. [Kilimanjaro Safaris](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/attractions/animal-kingdom/kilimanjaro-safaris/)
4. [Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/attractions/animal-kingdom/gorilla-falls-exploration-trail/)
5. [Flight of Passage](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/attractions/animal-kingdom/avatar-flight-of-passage/) — Single Pass timing determines exact placement
6. [Festival of the Lion King](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/entertainment/animal-kingdom/festival-of-the-lion-king/)
7. Walk Africa → Asia: allow 10–15 minutes
8. [Expedition Everest](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/attractions/animal-kingdom/expedition-everest/)
9. Optional [Maharajah Jungle Trek](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/attractions/animal-kingdom/maharajah-jungle-trek/)
10. Zootopia: Better Zoogether!
11. [Tiffins](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/animal-kingdom/tiffins/) at 1:00 p.m.
