# Orlando Family Vacation 2026

**Travel dates:** August 9–15, 2026  
**Planning status:** 🟡 Family review

## Vacation strategy

- Prioritize the family’s highest-rated experiences.
- Minimize unnecessary backtracking and long standby waits.
- Group attractions by park area.
- Preserve time for meals, shows, resort exploration, and swimming.
- Provide optional activities without turning every day into a race.
